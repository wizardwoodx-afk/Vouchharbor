# Vouch Harbor 19.5.0 "Authority" — upgrade notes

19.4.5 proved the accountable engine. 19.5.0 answers the five unsolved gaps
of the 2026 agent-identity landscape — with mechanics, not promises.

## The Authority Suite (new, probe-pinned — `src/vh19/authority.ts`)
1. **Mandate Passport (KYA)** — a signed, portable mandate: scope, budget
   ceiling that DECAYS as the agent operates, expiry, human owner signature.
   Missing, expired, ownerless or forged mandates are refusals, worded.
2. **Chain of Authority** — every delegation hop commits BOTH principals;
   scope can only SHRINK per hop (monotonic attenuation); depth is capped by
   the root mandate; tampered hops break the chain at the right place.
3. **Intent Receipts** — the agent declares intent IN WORDS before risky
   action; the gate approves the intent, not just the action; declared-vs-
   executed divergence is measured and receipted. We measure divergence; we
   never claim to read minds.
4. **Agent Warranty Pack** — the sealed assurance export: missions, approvals,
   refusals, intent-convergence rate, violations. The artifact insurers and
   contracts underwrite.
5. **Liability Map** — from a verified chain: who owed what at every hop,
   owner first.

## VOUCHMESH™ — bot-to-bot collaboration, made strong (new — `src/vh19/vouchMesh.ts`)
Bots don't trust each other. They vouch for each other — with receipts.
- Registered peers only; plain-http refused; tamper-evident identity digests.
- Mutual attestation: both peers sign each other; self-attestation refused.
- Joint receipts: every shared mission CO-SIGNED by all participants;
  outsiders cannot co-sign; either party verifies offline, forever.
- Compounding pair trust: probation → vouched → proven; divergence decays it.
- Quarantine with a receipt: a peer that breaks the mesh leaves it.
